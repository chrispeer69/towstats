The realistic number

Full capture will not happen; you cannot answer every offer and you will lose some you do answer. On the unanswered bucket alone — the part needing no capital — half capture is $20,860 a month, about $253,800 a year. Quarter capture is $10,430 a month.

Before you buy a truck

354 offers were declined as “Equipment Not Available”, but only one involved a heavy or medium-duty vehicle. 159 were light service — tire changes, jump starts, lockouts — where the label is standing in for “we don’t want this”. The real equipment question is the remaining 174 tows, not 354 jobs.

How this was measured, and what it can’t tell you

Figures come from Towbook’s own dispatch records for account 61343, pulled directly from the Request Log data feed — 2,996 offers between 29 June and 28 July 2026. Classification and counting are deterministic; no estimate or model sits between the record and the number.

  • Towbook sends no offer amounts. All 2,996 records carry a blank value. Every dollar figure here comes from the averages you supplied — Agero $65, Allstate $65, NSD $85, Allied $65 — applied to tow jobs. Urgent.ly’s $65 is an assumption; you did not give a figure. Gross revenue, no fuel, labour or truck cost deducted.
  • “Client withdrew” is reported separately, not as recoverable. Some of those 713 are genuine client cancellations; some are clients giving up while waiting on you. The feed cannot distinguish them, so the headline number excludes all of them. The true recoverable figure is higher than stated, not lower.
  • Response time is not the constraint. Urgent.ly allows fifteen minutes and 30% still go unanswered; Allstate allows under two and almost none do. Negotiating longer windows would not help.
  • One month, annualised. No seasonality is modelled. July includes a holiday weekend.
  • 278 fabricated test records were found mixed into the working database during verification and removed before these figures were produced. Nothing above includes them.

What now runs on its own

The monitoring system behind this report is live against your account. It signs in, pulls the Request Log, classifies every offer, and reports without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

  • Hourly — the text message that replaces a person sending you counts, plus a line when tows go unanswered.
  • Daily — what was lost yesterday, why, and what needs attention today.
  • Weekly — improvement recommendations, ranked, each carrying the number that justifies it.
  • Monthly — trend: which causes are growing, whether a change worked.

Alerts fire on unanswered tows, forming blind spots, decline-cause spikes and close-off candidates. New service types surface rather than being silently bucketed — it has already flagged Mobile Diagnostic and Storage Release, both accepted every time offered, and proposed rules for them. Proposals wait for your approval; the system is not permitted to change its own rules.
