This page is the one place those job counts are multiplied by the owner's own per-client average job values, because "we missed 975 jobs" and "that was fifty-six thousand dollars" are the same fact and only one of them gets acted on. This page is the most quotable thing in the system: its figures will end up in a conversation with a client, a bank or a buyer, and none of them may travel without the sentence that says they are gross estimates from an owner-supplied price list. The redundancy is the point -- a reader who prints one section still gets the caveat. IT UNDERSTATES, AND SAYS WHERE. A job with no configured client value contributes nothing at all rather than a guessed default, so `unpriced_jobs` is printed beside the total and the clients responsible are named.
